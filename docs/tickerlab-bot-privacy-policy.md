# TickerLab Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

TickerLab Bot is a ticker analysis assistant that may provide market summaries, signal labels, confidence scores, risk notes, and related analysis outputs. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use TickerLab Bot.

By using TickerLab Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We May Process

When you interact with TickerLab Bot, we may process the following information:

- Telegram user ID, username, first name, and basic profile information provided by Telegram.
- Messages, ticker symbols, commands, files, or other content you send to the bot.
- Chat ID, message ID, timestamps, and conversation metadata.
- Analysis requests and generated bot responses.
- Technical logs related to bot operation, security, errors, and performance.

Do not send sensitive personal information, passwords, API keys, payment details, confidential documents, or private financial account information to the bot.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information processed by TickerLab Bot to:

- Provide ticker analysis and related bot responses.
- Improve report quality, bot behavior, and user experience.
- Monitor reliability, performance, and security.
- Debug errors and maintain the service.
- Prevent abuse, misuse, spam, or unauthorized access.

## 3. Financial and Investment Disclaimer

TickerLab Bot may generate ticker analysis, market summaries, signal labels, confidence scores, or risk notes. These outputs are for informational purposes only.

TickerLab Bot does not provide financial, investment, legal, tax, or trading advice. You are responsible for your own financial decisions and should consult a qualified professional before making investment or trading decisions.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure, hosting, logging, analytics, market data, AI, or messaging providers used to operate the bot. These providers may process information only as needed to deliver their services, maintain the bot, or support service operations.

Telegram separately processes data according to Telegram's own privacy practices.

## 5. Data Retention

We aim to retain information only as long as reasonably needed to provide the bot, maintain security, improve quality, debug issues, comply with legal obligations, or prevent abuse.

Some data may be deleted earlier during routine maintenance, deployments, or system updates.

## 6. Access and Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of TickerLab Bot by contacting the bot owner or project administrator.

Some information may be retained if needed for security, legal, abuse prevention, debugging, or operational reasons.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect bot data. However, no system is perfectly secure. You should avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through Telegram messages.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TickerLab Bot is not intended for children. Users should not use this bot if they are not old enough to use Telegram and understand this Privacy Policy.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TickerLab Bot changes. The latest version should be reviewed before using the bot.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the owner or administrator of TickerLab Bot.
