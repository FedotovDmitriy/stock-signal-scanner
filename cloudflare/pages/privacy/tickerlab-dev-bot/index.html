TickerLab Dev Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

TickerLab Dev Bot is a private development and testing bot. It is not a final public product, and its outputs may be experimental, incomplete, or unstable.

1. Information We May Process

When you interact with TickerLab Dev Bot, we may process the following information:

  • Telegram user ID, username, first name, and basic profile information provided by Telegram.
  • Messages, ticker symbols, commands, files, or other content you send to the bot.
  • Chat ID, message ID, timestamps, and conversation metadata.
  • Technical logs related to bot operation, debugging, errors, and performance.
  • Analysis requests and generated bot responses.

Do not send sensitive personal information, private financial information, passwords, API keys, payment details, or confidential documents to this development bot.

2. How We Use Information

We use information processed by TickerLab Dev Bot to test ticker analysis features, improve report formatting, debug errors, monitor stability, prevent abuse, and maintain development or quality assurance records.

TickerLab Dev Bot is intended for internal testing and development purposes only.

3. Financial and Investment Disclaimer

TickerLab Dev Bot may generate ticker analysis, market summaries, signal labels, confidence scores, or risk notes. These outputs are for testing and informational purposes only.

The bot does not provide financial, investment, legal, tax, or trading advice. You should not make financial decisions based only on outputs from this development bot.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure, hosting, logging, analytics, market data, AI, or messaging providers used to operate and test the bot. Telegram separately processes data according to Telegram's own privacy practices.

5. Data Retention

Because TickerLab Dev Bot is a development environment, logs and test data may be retained for debugging, quality assurance, and product improvement.

We aim to keep development data only as long as reasonably needed for testing, debugging, security, or operational purposes. Some data may be deleted earlier during routine maintenance, deployments, or environment resets.

6. Access and Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of TickerLab Dev Bot by contacting the bot owner or project administrator.

Some information may be retained if needed for security, legal, debugging, abuse prevention, or operational reasons.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect bot data. However, no system is perfectly secure. Because this is a development bot, you should treat all interactions as test activity and avoid sending sensitive or confidential information.

8. Children's Privacy

TickerLab Dev Bot is not intended for children. Users should not use this bot if they are not old enough to use Telegram and understand this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TickerLab Dev Bot changes. The latest version should be reviewed before using the bot.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the owner or administrator of TickerLab Dev Bot.
